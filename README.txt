ROYAL PRINCESS DAY SURPRISE
===========================

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the folder "royal_princess_day".
3. Double-click index.html. It opens in Chrome, Edge, Safari, etc.
4. For the best tablet experience, rotate the tablet to landscape if you like.

HOW TO ADD YOUR 5–7 PHOTOS
1. Open the "photos" folder.
2. Add your pictures and name them exactly:
   photo1.jpg
   photo2.jpg
   photo3.jpg
   photo4.jpg
   photo5.jpg
   photo6.jpg
   photo7.jpg
3. If you have fewer than 7, the unused slides will show a placeholder.
4. JPG files are recommended. If your photos are PNG, either convert them to JPG
   or update the matching file extension in script.js.

HOW TO PERSONALISE
- Open index.html in a text editor.
- Search for [Write your own paragraph here...] and replace it with your message.
- Update the other letter text and final message as you wish.
- In script.js, edit PHOTO_CAPTIONS to add captions for your photos.

HOST ONLINE
- Upload the extracted project folder contents to a static hosting service.
- Keep index.html, style.css, script.js and the photos folder together.
- You can also open index.html locally without hosting.
- Google Fonts are optional; the page uses fallback fonts if offline.

No frameworks, build step, backend, or paid service required.

NEW FUN FEATURES
- Three-question affectionate quiz.
- Reveal the 'I love you / I love you, Mr Cool' memory.
- Collect five hearts for a little celebration.
- Your expanded personal message appears in the final section.
